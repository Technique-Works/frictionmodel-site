# The Friction Model ## Messaging Kit **Technique Works · Internal · Working Draft v0.1** --- Everything written for or about The Friction Model draws from this document. One message, one register, consistent across every surface. Copy from here rather than writing fresh — inconsistency across platforms is much harder to unpick than to prevent. --- ## 1 · The core ### The positioning statement > Every industrial operation runs two systems. The one that is written down, and the one that actually happens. > > The distance between them is where incidents live. > > The Friction Model closes it. Not by asking people to try harder — by moving the friction. Out of the correct action, into the dangerous one. This is the argument. Everything else is a compression of it. ### Tagline **Primary: Move the friction.** Three words, states the mechanism, and it is the only claim in the industry that says friction should be relocated rather than eliminated. Alternates, if the primary is taken or does not sit right: - *Friction belongs somewhere.* - *Where the written system meets the real one.* - *The correct way, made easier.* ### Descriptor **A risk, compliance and governance framework for heavy industry. From Technique Works.** Used wherever the name needs immediate context — bios, footers, first mention in an article. ### Relationship to the parent brand The Friction Model is a Technique Works framework. Never presented as a separate company. Standard construction: **"The Friction Model, from Technique Works"** or **"a Technique Works framework."** Never: "Friction Model Ltd", "Friction Model BV", or anything implying a separate entity. `[POLICY]` --- ## 2 · The length ladder Use the shortest version that fits the space. **Three words** > Move the friction. **Ten words** > Closing the gap between the written system and the real operation. **Twenty-five words** > A framework for building HSEQ management systems in heavy industry. It closes the gap between what procedures say and what actually happens on the floor. **Fifty words** > Every industrial operation runs two systems: the one written down and the one that actually happens. The Friction Model closes the distance between them — removing resistance from the correct action and adding it to the dangerous shortcut. Six phases, binary gates, a system that runs after we leave. **One hundred and fifty words** > Every industrial operation runs two systems. The one that is written down — procedures, permits, the management system as it appears in the document register. And the one that actually happens at three in the morning on the third night of a turnaround. > > The distance between them is where incidents live. > > Most organisations respond by restating the first system more loudly. More training, more signage, more reminders that the procedure exists. This treats the gap as an information problem. It rarely is. People know the procedure. They are not following it because something makes following it harder than not following it. > > That something is friction, and friction can be moved. Out of the correct action, so compliance is the path of least resistance. Into the shortcut, so the wrong move costs something. > > The Friction Model is how that gets built. From Technique Works. --- ## 3 · Platform copy Character counts are approximate limits at time of writing — verify before pasting. ### LinkedIn — company page **Tagline** *(120 char limit)* > Move the friction. Never certified, accredited or approved - **Named third-party methodologies.** Public standards by clause only - **Client names** without written authorisation - **The banned vocabulary** — see the voice profile - **"Safety first," "zero harm" without context, "compliance is key"** - Claims that the founder personally delivers every engagement ### The register Confident, direct, problem-solving. It describes a problem the reader already recognises, more precisely than they could describe it themselves. It does not sell a solution they have never heard of. Credentials appear as the result they produced, never as the achievement itself. --- ## 6 · The holding page Until the full site exists, frictionmodel.com carries a single page with the positioning statement, the descriptor, a link to Technique Works, and nothing else. No countdown, no "coming soon," no email capture. A page that states an idea clearly is worth more than a page that promises one later. --- ## 7 · Logo direction For Abdullah.
